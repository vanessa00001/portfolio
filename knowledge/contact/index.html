What I can help with

Transformation that has stalled

A digital transformation initiative that started well and is now stuck between teams, budgets, or decisions nobody wants to own.

ERP, CRM, and AI programs needing direction

Programs where the technology is capable but the strategic direction, scope, or sequencing has not been settled.

Systems that do not work as one

Enterprise platforms that need integration and process optimization, where data is re-keyed, reconciled, or quietly trusted less each quarter.

Complex delivery needing experienced leadership

Multi-workstream programs that need a senior owner who stays close enough to the work to clear blockers personally.

Ways to work together

Four collaboration models. Which one fits depends on the problem and how your organization prefers to bring senior help in.

Strategic Advisory

Senior counsel for a leader carrying a transformation, ERP, or operations agenda. An outside perspective on the decisions that carry real consequence.

Fractional Project Leadership

Senior ownership of a specific program, from plan through go-live and adoption, without the cost of a permanent hire.

Digital Transformation Consulting

End to end ownership of a transformation, from discovery and design through delivery, adoption, and the governance that keeps it in place.

AI & Automation Strategy

An evidence-led view of where automation and AI create value, where they do not, and what has to be true before either is worth building.

Starting a conversation

Questions people ask first

Do you work with organizations outside the Philippines?
Yes. Delivery has spanned North America, EMEA, LATAM, and APAC, and engagements are run remotely with travel where a program genuinely needs it.
Can you work with existing internal teams or consulting partners?
Usually, yes, and it is often the better arrangement. I work alongside internal teams and existing partners, taking ownership of the parts that need senior attention rather than displacing people who already know the business.
What size of engagement makes sense?
Anything from a time-boxed discovery through to full ownership of a transformation program. The deciding factor is the consequence of the decision, not the budget attached to it.
Do you work hourly?
No. Engagements are scoped to an outcome and priced after a short conversation, so the incentive stays on the result rather than the time spent.
What happens after I get in touch?
A short conversation about the problem, with no obligation on either side. If it is a fit, a scoped proposal follows. If it is not, I will say so and point you somewhere more useful.
