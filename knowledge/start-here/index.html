Insights

Automate the Toil, Not the Judgment

Automation fails when it creeps into decisions that need judgment. The Toil-Judgment Line shows what to automate, what to assist, and what to keep human.

Insights

Requirements Are Decisions, Not Documents

Requirements fail after sign-off because a document records what was agreed, not why. The RDR Model reframes them as durable decision records that cut rework.

Insights

The Status Report That Tells the Truth

Status theater hides trouble until recovery is expensive. The Evidence-Backed Status model pairs every claim with evidence, trend, and a decision.
