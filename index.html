Automation · AI + n8n + Workato · Kairion Group

AI-powered order-to-cash automation

Problem
Orders came in through email, e-commerce, EDI, and a web portal, and operations re-keyed each one across CRM, ERP, inventory, and fulfillment, coordinating by email and updating status by hand. Depending on complexity, each order took 10 to 15 minutes of manual work, invited errors, and tied the team up in routine processing.
Action
n8n monitors incoming orders, reads PDF and email purchase orders, and uses AI and OCR to extract customer details, SKUs, quantities, shipping addresses, delivery dates, and payment terms, then validates and de-duplicates into a structured record. Workato creates or updates the customer in the CRM, creates the sales order in the ERP, checks and reserves inventory, triggers fulfillment, sends shipping notifications and confirmations, and routes exceptions like stock shortages or pricing gaps to the right team. It also refreshes real-time executive dashboards and sends an AI summary of risks and delayed orders to leadership by email or Microsoft Teams.
Result
For organizations processing several hundred orders a day, eliminates thousands of manual processing hours a year, improves order accuracy, and gives leadership real-time visibility, while operations focus on exceptions instead of routine entry.
Stack
n8n as the AI and document layer; Workato as the governed enterprise orchestration across CRM, ERP, fulfillment, and reporting.
Two-layer design: n8n reads and structures each order, Workato orchestrates CRM, ERP, fulfillment, and reporting.
Automation · AI Agents · Kairion Group

AI customer-support automation

Problem

Action

Result

Support flow: inbound channels triaged by an AI layer, with human escalation reserved for complex cases.

Automation · n8n + Workato · Finance · Kairion Group

Accounts-payable invoice processing

Problem
Finance staff opened every invoice, keyed the data, checked the purchase order, sent approval emails, updated the ERP, and tracked payment status in spreadsheets, roughly 10 to 20 minutes per invoice.
Action
n8n reads each invoice from email, a shared folder, or a vendor portal and uses AI to extract the vendor, invoice and PO numbers, dates, line items, tax, currency, and total, flagging missing data, duplicates, and unusual values. Workato then validates the supplier against the ERP, matches the invoice to the purchase order and goods receipt, routes exceptions for approval, creates the invoice record, updates payment status, notifies finance or the supplier, and refreshes the executive dashboard.
Result
At 3,000 invoices a month, cutting even 10 minutes of manual work per invoice frees around 500 labor hours a month, roughly $12,500 monthly or $150,000 a year at a $25 hourly cost, before further savings from fewer duplicate payments, fewer late fees, and faster approvals.
Stack
n8n as the AI and document-processing layer; Workato as the governed workflow across the ERP, approvals, notifications, and reporting.
Two-layer design: n8n extracts and validates invoices, Workato runs supplier check, PO match, approvals, ERP posting, and reporting.

Reporting flow: source systems consolidate on a schedule, an AI layer refreshes dashboards and writes a plain-language summary for leadership.
